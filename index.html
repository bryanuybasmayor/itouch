About I-touch

A quiet sanctuary,
held by healing hands

Tucked into the heart of Melfort, I-touch Massage Spa Clinic was created by Roden Ancheta, a Registered Massage Therapist (CMMOTA Licensed No. 5057), as a place to slow down. Soft light, warm towels, the faint scent of essential oils — from the moment you step inside, the world outside gets quieter.

Every treatment begins with a conversation. Roden listens first, then works with skilled, intuitive hands to release what your body has been holding — from deep tissue and therapeutic work to deeply calming relaxation massage. Whether you arrive with chronic pain or simply the need to rest, you leave feeling lighter, looser and truly cared for.

  • Personalized sessions
  • Private, calming rooms
